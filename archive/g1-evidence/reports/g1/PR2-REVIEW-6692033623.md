## 1. Review findings verified

The original sense network was 3.59 kΩ / 1.00 kΩ directly from `VRAW` to the TLV3601 input, with no series resistor. Its ideal divider node is 8.4361 V at 38.7219 V and 8.8691 V at 40.709 V. TI states the TLV3601 input is diode-clamped to VCC/GND, recommends common-mode only from VEE−0.2 V to VCC+0.2 V, gives VCC+0.3 V as the input absolute limit, and requires rail-overdrive current to be limited to 10 mA. Thus the commenter’s clamp concern is valid; the pin cannot be treated as a high-impedance divider input at the fault voltage. [TI TLV360x Rev E, pp. 5–7](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv3601.pdf).

Using the datasheet-boundary clamp approximation `Vpin = VCC + 0.3 V`, divider KCL reproduces the current-injection concern:

| VRAW | VCC | Calculated TLV3601 clamp current | 10 mA datasheet condition |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 38.7219 V | 0 V | 10.402 mA | **FAIL** |
| 40.709 V | 0 V | 10.956 mA | **FAIL** |
| 38.7219 V | 5 V | 4.010 mA | under limit; rail sink/rise still unproved |
| 40.709 V | 5 V | 4.563 mA | under limit; rail sink/rise still unproved |

At VCC=0, the model has no clamp-current sink or rail state. The independent review correctly identified both possible input-current violation and 5 V back-power. The original 40.709 V eFuse-IN exposure and its 731.759 µs above 24 V / 161.124 µs above 30 V remain unchanged; see [prior review and source log](PR2-REVIEW-6683120653.md).

## 2. Candidate detector/bias arrangement

**HESAP / candidate only:** keep `TLV3601DCKR` as the fast OVP path, but add `TLV1851DBVR` as an independent high-voltage/fail-safe fault and ARM inhibit. Both use the 17.95 kΩ / 5.00 kΩ divider ratio (3.59:1) and the existing `REF5025AQDRQ1` 2.5 V reference. Connect the 5 V analog/latch supply to the *raw buck output ahead of the common isolator* using `TPS7A1650QDGNRQ1`; retain the separate 12 V LTC7001 driver rail from battery-side VIN with `TPS7A1601QDGNRQ1`. Add a 470 Ω rail bleeder. Tie the LDO power-good, comparator fault outputs (through current-limiting isolation resistors for push-pull outputs), and the deliberate reset policy into the latch’s asynchronous active-low clear. A manual ARM edge is allowed only after `VRAW` is below the valid OVP threshold and the rails are good. The exact clear/ARM resistor network remains a schematic-phase input, not a verified circuit.

The high-speed TLV3601 divider is deliberately raised from 3.59 kΩ / 1 kΩ to 17.95 kΩ / 5 kΩ. The ratio is unchanged, but clamp current falls and input resistor power falls. Resistor MPNs/footprints are **not selected**. With a 0.1%, 25 ppm/K class assumed, RTOP power under an unpowered 40.709 V clamp is about 0.091 W; a 1206 0.25 W class would be below 50% nominal rating, subject to exact MPN/temperature derating verification.

The official TI orderable addendum lists `TLV1851DBVR` as active production, SOT-23-5. Inventory was not checked. TLV1851 inputs are specified fail-safe from −0.1 V to 40 V independently of V+, are not diode-clamped to V+, and tolerate high inputs while V+ is unpowered. Its POR holds the push-pull output low during startup for up to 2 ms after the 1.5 V POR point. [TI TLV185x Rev B, pp. 8–11, 16, 19](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv1851.pdf). In the shared-node candidate, TLV3601 itself clamps the sense node to about 5.3 V at a powered 5 V rail; that is still a valid over-rail input for TLV1851 and remains above the 2.5 V reference.

**Limit:** the TLV3601 input is still 0.1 V beyond its recommended common-mode ceiling when clamped at VCC+0.3 V. Its output state outside recommended common-mode is not used as the sustained-fault safety proof: the independent TLV1851 over-rail path and fail-clear latch policy must hold OFF. The TLV1851 datasheet plots are typical and provide no guaranteed maximum comparator propagation delay for this use. It is an ARM/startup interlock only; it cannot be credited as the fast O1 protector. The TLV3601’s 4.5 ns maximum is conditional on 50 mV overdrive and valid common-mode. Real fault slew/overdrive and latch/driver/FET end-to-end timing remain **AÇIK**.

## 4. Separate capacitor currents and charge balance

KCL residuals: common-node −0.0005 µC, O1 branch 0 µC, S2E branch −0.0001 µC. The current division confirms that the common switch branch current does not all charge `CEFIN`; the two input capacitors discharge slightly in this run while output capacitors supply load. The negative U4/eFuse branch charge is an ideal bilateral-switch artifact; TPS259824O reverse-current behavior is not modeled or established by this number. This updates the earlier uniform `I·t/C` screen but does **not** establish physical Ceff, ESR/ESL, reverse-current behavior, or TPS25982 transient immunity.
