## 1. Independent verification

The prior report's 470-ohm rail calculation gives about 0.964 V for the idealized unpowered rail corner. That is below the SN74LVC1G74 minimum 1.65 V operating supply, but it does **not** establish a safe latch output: below 1.65 V, the latch's state and outputs are outside its specified operating range. The previous rail test has been renamed and explicitly scoped to the rail voltage only. The LTC7001 input and gates were absent from the prior rail-loss deck.

The prior charge-balance deck uses `EFUSESW`, an ideal bilateral voltage-controlled switch. `Sefuse_S2E` can conduct in either direction; `Btrip_EF2` only reacts to `VSAFE > 16.32 V`. The logged `Q_EF2=-44.547 uC` therefore verifies only that behavioral netlist. It is not evidence for TPS259824O backfeed, output hold-up, reverse blocking, or protection.

Manufacturer documents checked:

- [TI SN74LVC1G74 Rev G](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g74.pdf), pp. 4, 7, 10: DCU pin map, 1.65-5.5 V operating range, asynchronous CLR/PRE function table, and nonstable PRE/CLR-low state.
- [ADI LTC7001 Rev G](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/ltc7001.pdf), pp. 3, 7, 9-10: supply UVLO thresholds, pin assignments, INP thresholds and internal 1 Mohm pull-down, TGDN behavior, and gate output resistance/timing conditions.
- [TI TPS25982 Rev D](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps25982.pdf), pp. 1, 6-7, 20-21: 2.7-24 V recommended IN, 30 V absolute maximum IN, OUT maximum bounded by `VIN+0.3 V`, and no reverse-current blocking guarantee found in the datasheet.
- [TI TPS7A16-Q1 Rev E](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps7a16-q1.pdf), pp. 3-5, 10-11: DGN-8 pin map/PG pin 3, 3-60 V input, 66.2 C/W JEDEC-board theta-JA, operating Tj up to 150 C, rising thermal shutdown at 170 C, PG behavior and its VIN>=3 V validity condition.
- [TI CSD19536KTT Rev C](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/csd19536ktt.pdf), pp. 1-3, 5-6: 100 V VDS, +/-20 V VGS, 118 nC typical Qg at 10 V, and SOA data.
- [TI TLV3601 Rev E](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv3601.pdf), pp. 5-7: input common-mode, clamp, and propagation-delay conditions.

## 2. Pin-level control contract

This section defines the required logic; it is not a claim that the proposed chain is already wired. No matching latch/driver/FET circuit or component SPICE models are present in the candidate evidence. The machine-readable pin map and state rows are in [fail-safe-audit-results.json](fail-safe-audit-results.json).

### Latch and ARM pins

For the SN74LVC1G74DCU package: `CLK=1`, `D=2`, `Qbar=3`, `GND=4`, `Q=5`, active-low `CLR=6`, active-low `PRE=7`, and `VCC=8`.

Required connection contract:

1. Keep `PRE_n` high at all times. The datasheet says `PRE_n=0` and `CLR_n=0` is nonstable and drives both outputs high; it is prohibited as a reset policy.
2. Tie `D` high. A rising edge on `CLK` is the only operation that sets `Q=1`; qualify the manual ARM edge with all safety conditions.
3. Assert `CLR_n=0` for any OVP, logic PG loss, unhealthy monitor, or driver inhibit. This asynchronously clears `Q=0`. Do not rely on `Q` when `VCC<1.65 V`.
4. After a fault clears, keep `CLK` low. `Q` remains zero until a new permitted manual ARM edge; there is no automatic restart.
5. For the TPS7A1650QDGNRQ1 DGN-8 candidate, `PG=pin 3`, `DELAY=pin 7`, `IN=pin 8`, and `GND=pin 4`. PG is open-drain active-high: it pulls low when VOUT is below its falling threshold (83-93% of nominal) and its PG function is valid only for LDO VIN>=3 V. Its output/deassert timing has no guaranteed maximum used in this analysis. The pull-up, receiving logic, fault-combiner, and reset network still need electrical implementation.

### LTC7001 and both FET gates

`LTC7001 INP` is pin 4, with VIH 1.7-2.2 V, VIL 1.3-1.8 V, and an internal 1 Mohm pull-down. Add an external 10 kohm INP-to-GND pull-down so a genuinely high-impedance command is low. The LTC7001's `VCCUV` pin 2 tied to ground selects a 3.1-3.7 V rising UVLO and 2.8-3.4 V falling UVLO; the BST-TS falling UVLO is 2.8-3.1 V. With valid driver rails and INP low, TGDN pin 6 pulls the gate node to TS. On UVLO, TGDN is also pulled to TS.

For each CSD19536KTTT in D2PAK, `G=pin 1`, `D=pin 2`, and `S=pin 3` (the tab is also drain). Provide a separate gate-to-source bleed from pin 1 to pin 3 on **each** FET; do not reference the floating source pin to system ground. These resistors are not in the audited fixture or a verified schematic path. They are needed to discharge stored gate charge if the driver itself loses power; their value and discharge time remain OPEN. The datasheet gives 118 nC typical / 153 nC maximum gate charge per device under its stated 10 V, 50 V VDS, 100 A test condition; that does not establish the actual two-FET turn-off time in this circuit. The LTC7001 70 ns maximum propagation row is for a 1 nF load; it does not bound the two-FET turn-off. No physical `VGS1`, `VGS2`, drain current, or SOA evidence exists.

The existing candidate does not show an independent, powered driver-side clamp from `LTC7001 INP` to GND when the 5 V logic rail is invalid. Add such an inhibit, or prove a specified PG/CLR implementation keeps INP low throughout the rail-loss window. The 5 V LDO's PG alone is not assumed valid after its own input falls below 3 V. The proposed 5 V source from protected battery VIN (20-26 V) keeps the detector rail independent of the buck-output fault, but the monitor/clamp MPN, pin wiring, and timing remain unselected/unverified. The detector chain must assert `CLR_n` before the TLV3601 leaves valid common-mode; the current TLV1851 candidate has no guaranteed maximum delay for this trip path.

### Negative fault-injection fixture

The new [ngspice fixture](sim/ovp-control-loss-negative-fixture.cir) deliberately forces a stuck-high INP command as the 5 V logic rail is lost while the 12 V driver remains. This is an adversarial input injection, not a SN74LVC1G74 or LTC7001 macromodel. Without a separate clamp, measured INP is 4.541 V and both idealized gates remain 9.996 V. With an ideal driver-side clamp, INP is about 0.001 V and both modeled gates discharge to numerical zero. It confirms the required logic dominance in this fixture only; it does not select or verify a real supervisor, timing, MOSFET, or hardware path. ## 3. TPS259824O input loss and reverse current

The TI datasheet's absolute maximum for OUT is `min(30 V, VIN+0.3 V)`; recommended OUT is also bounded relative to VIN. If a port's output capacitor remains at 11 V after IN falls to 0 V, that hypothetical state exceeds the stated OUT maximum by 10.7 V. This does **not** prove the actual part reaches or tolerates that state: the output-capacitor discharge curve, reverse current, and power-off pass-FET path are not specified as reverse-current blocking behavior in the reviewed datasheet. It means the precharged-output/input-loss case cannot be assumed safe.

The old `-44.547 uC` S2E branch value is excluded as physical evidence. The old behavioral netlist uses an ideal bilateral `SW`, no device UVLO/output-discharge or internal FET model, and its trip only watches input overvoltage. The voltage constraint above is DATASHEET; the actual device transient is OPEN. Use vendor clarification or a guarded bench test with separate VIN/OUT current measurement before claiming reverse isolation.

## 4. TPS7A16 thermal scenarios and source choice

The 85 C ambient remains an assumption. The prior analysis used 125 C as if it were a TI reliability limit; this was incorrect. TI's datasheet lists operating Tj through 150 C and thermal shutdown rising at 170 C. The 125 C number is retained only as a conservative engineering screen. The 66.2 C/W theta-JA is a JEDEC board result and not the actual PCB.

For the same 19.171 mA 5 V load screen, using VOUT=4.9 V for pass-device loss, 15 uA LDO IQ as a light-load sensitivity, and 66.2 C/W:

| LDO source and condition | P estimate | Tj at assumed Ta=85 C | Status |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| VRAW=11 V sustained normal | 0.117 W | 92.75 C | below 125 C screen in JEDEC-board estimate |
| VRAW=26 V sustained fault | 0.405 W | 111.80 C | below 125 C screen in JEDEC-board estimate |
| VRAW=40.709 V sustained hypothetical | 0.687 W | 130.49 C | **FAIL against conservative 125 C screen**; below 150 C datasheet max only under theta estimate |
| Protected VIN=20 V sustained | 0.290 W | 104.18 C | below 125 C screen in JEDEC-board estimate |
| Protected VIN=26 V sustained | 0.405 W | 111.80 C | below 125 C screen in JEDEC-board estimate |
| VRAW=40.709 V short fault pulse | not bounded | Tj peak OPEN | exact pulse width and TPS7A16 transient Ztheta(t) absent |

The 470 ohm bleeder draws up to 10.961 mA and dissipates about 55.9 mW at 5.1 V and its -1% resistance corner. Its exact resistor rating and board temperature derating are OPEN. Protected VIN is the better source candidate for the 5 V detector/latch rail: it raises normal dissipation versus 11 V VRAW but remains independent of the buck-output surge and screens to 111.8 C at 26 V under the stated assumptions. It is not accepted until actual board theta, source transients, PG behavior, and rail-loss ordering are tested.
