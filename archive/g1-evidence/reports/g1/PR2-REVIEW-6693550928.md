## Rail-loss measurements

TI lists `CSD19536KTTT` maximum gate charge as 153 nC at the datasheet test point, giving the **screening proxy** `Ceq = Qg/VGS = 15.3 nF` per FET. This is not a MOSFET macromodel or a guaranteed switching capacitance. The modeled devices have 10 V initial VGS; `VGS1` and `VGS2` are each measured against their common-source `TS=26 V`.

| Fixture arm | Rail event and modeled path | VGS at +0.5 µs | VGS at +1 µs | Time to 2.1 V | Classification |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Active-discharge screen | 5 V and 12 V ramp from nominal to 0 V over 10 µs; ideal 4 Ω TGDN-to-TS sink is credited only while driver VCC exceeds 3.4 V | 0.161969 V | 0.002605 V | 0.1894 µs after fault start | **OPEN.** 4 Ω maximum is specified only with VCC and BST−TS at 12 V; the linear Qg proxy and ideal sink do not establish the real LTC7001 two-gate discharge or driver UVLO behavior. |
| Abrupt dual-rail loss, passive only | 5 V and 12 V collapse at fault start; no active driver sink credited; each FET has 10 kΩ G-S bleed | 9.96734 V | — | 238.779 µs | **FAIL** against the earlier 0.5 µs diagnostic turn-off screen; **OPEN** for physical device behavior. At +100 µs VGS is still 5.20171 V. |

The passive RC cross-check uses two 15.3 nF proxies in parallel and two 10 kΩ bleeds in parallel: `Req=5 kΩ`, `Ceq,total=30.6 nF`, `τ=153 µs`, and `t(10→2.1 V)=τ·ln(10/2.1)=238.7791 µs`. The ngspice result differs by less than 0.01%. Meeting a 0.5 µs RC screen with only two equal passive bleeds would require about 20.9 Ω each; at 10 V each would draw about 0.48 A and dissipate about 4.8 W while the FET is on. This is a scale check, not a proposed resistor value. The active-sink arm is likewise a conditional model screen, not a pass.

## eFuse OUT−IN diagnostic

The TI TPS25982 absolute-maximum OUT condition is `OUT ≤ min(30 V, VIN+0.3 V)`. The behavioral fixture assumes 20 µF output capacitance per port, 400 nF total local input capacitance, a 100 Ω input bleed, an O1 100 kΩ light-load assumption, and an S2E 54.5 Ω resistive load derived from the manufacturer's 12 V / 0.22 A working-current datum. The resistor load is not an S2E dynamic model; actual TPS259824O reverse-current/pass-FET behavior is unmodeled.

| Corner | Port | Max modeled OUT−IN | Time above 0.3 V | Meaning |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Common disconnect opens; eFuse paths forced open; outputs precharged to 11 V | O1 | 10.9971 V | ≥11.099 ms, right-censored by 12 ms window | Conditional fixture exceeds the datasheet boundary; silicon response **OPEN**. |
| Same common disconnect corner | S2E | 9.36228 V | 3.921 ms | Conditional fixture exceeds the boundary; silicon response **OPEN**. |
| Ideal bilateral eFuse paths remain on | O1 / S2E | 0.000155 V / approximately 0 V | 0 ms | Reverse-feed masking artifact only; expressly not valid eFuse evidence. |
| Separate precharged output with VIN forced to 0 V and eFuse path open | O1 | 11.000 V | ≥12.000 ms, right-censored | Conditional fixture exceeds the boundary; silicon response **OPEN**. |
| Same input-loss corner | S2E | 10.999 V | 3.922 ms | Conditional fixture exceeds the boundary; silicon response **OPEN**. |

The S2E RC estimate is 3.926 ms versus 3.922 ms in the same fixture (−0.105%). O1's 100 kΩ case does not fall below 0.3 V before the simulation ends; the reported duration is only a lower bound. None of these are predicted TPS259824O pin waveforms or reverse currents.

## One pin-level electrical candidate

This replaces the earlier 5 V `TLV3601DCKR` sense candidate. Its divider would put 8.869 V on the comparator input at VRAW=40.709 V. Against a 5 V supply, that is 3.569 V above the TLV3601's VCC+0.3 V absolute-maximum input boundary; the pin clamp is not a valid functional operating mode. The replacement uses a 12 V powered wide-common-mode automotive comparator. The drawing/netlist remains an analysis candidate; it has not been wired into KiCad or validated as fail-safe.

| Function / pins | Candidate connection |
|---|---|
| OVP sense: `U_OVP TLV1701QDBVRQ1`, SOT-23-5 | Pin 1 IN+ ← 2.5 V reference; pin 2 V− → GND; pin 3 IN− ← VRAW divider; pin 4 open-collector OUT → `CLR_n`; pin 5 V+ ← 12 V OVP rail. Pin 4 sinks `CLR_n` when IN− exceeds IN+. |
| Reference: `U_REF REF5025AQDRQ1`, SOIC-8 | Pin 2 VIN ← regulated 5 V; pin 3 TEMP left unconnected; pin 4 GND; pin 5 TRIM/NR bypassed with 1 µF to GND; pin 6 VOUT → U_OVP pin 1; pin 7 NC, pins 1/8 DNC. The reference output is 2.5 V nominal. |
| Divider | VRAW → 17.95 kΩ top resistor → U_OVP pin 3 / 5.00 kΩ bottom resistor → GND. Nominal trip is `2.5·(1+17.95/5)=11.475 V`. At 11.1605 V, nominal input is 2.43148 V; at 40.709 V, 8.86906 V. These are nominal resistor/reference calculations only. |
| Regulated rails and PG | `U_5V TPS7A1650QDGNRQ1` pin 8 IN ← protected battery VIN; pin 1 OUT → latch/reference supply; pin 3 PG → wired low-dominant `PG_OK`. `U_12V TPS7A1601QDGNRQ1` pin 8 IN ← protected battery VIN; pin 1 OUT → U_OVP pin 5 and LTC7001 VCC; pins 3 PG → same `PG_OK`. PRE_n is never asserted low. |

| INP rail-loss clamp | `Q_INP_CLAMP` and `Q_INP_RELEASE`, both `MMBT3904LT1G` SOT-23: pin 1 base, pin 2 emitter, pin 3 collector. Clamp collector → INP, emitter → GND, base → 12 V through 22 kΩ. Release transistor collector → clamp base, emitter → GND, base ← PG_OK through 82 kΩ. Candidate values only; transistor gain corners, pull-down level and fail-safe state with both rails absent are unverified. |

The sequence table starts with `Q=None` and advances cold-start → reset with PG asserted → PG-valid/disarmed → ARM rising edge → OVP fault while ARM is held → fault recovery while still held → release → fresh ARM press. Resulting Q is `[unknown, 0, 0, 1, 0, 0, 0, 1]`; a held button does not re-arm. This is a logical transition contract, not a transistor-level or latch macromodel run.

**Datasheet limits/open items:** The TLV1701-Q1 is specified for 2.2–36 V supply and rail-to-rail inputs; its 460/560 ns propagation entries are not a guaranteed maximum in the cited test table. The 40.709 V divider node is nominally 8.869 V under a nominal 12 V rail, but resistor/reference tolerances, comparator offset/noise, min rail, TVS-clamped VRAW, and propagation maximum are not closed. `U3/U4` reverse current and output decay remain undocumented by this fixture. The 10 kΩ gate bleed fails the 0.5 µs screen when both supplies disappear. This candidate therefore remains **OPEN/BLOCKED**, not a fail-safe design acceptance.
