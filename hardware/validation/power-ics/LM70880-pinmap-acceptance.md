Polygon boundaries were extracted from the manufacturer PDF vector drawing, calibrated using15:1 scale and documented -3.2 mm datum. Curved edges were sampled, endpoint gaps joined within0.01 PDF point, then decomposed into0.005-mm strips with0.002-mm overlaps. Copper shape Hausdorff deviation is below0.007 mm and area excess below0.35%; see rrx-geometry-evidence.json. This is an explicit approximation, not an exact manufacturer CAD footprint. The lower-left VIN paste aperture is conservatively narrower to stay inside the recommended chamfer; production stencil review is required. No production qualification is claimed.
